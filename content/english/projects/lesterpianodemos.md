## 100 year old Lester Piano 

What can be pulled out of a hundred year old, somewhat out of tune and dead piano?  All of my life I have wanted an acoustic piano.   A year after getting an old upright, and self teaching myself how to play, these demos were created.   One a day, recorded with morning coffee, first take - minutes after composition.  I lasted 17 days.   

Are they perfect? No

Mistakes?  Debatable

Pure Creative Flow?  Absolutely

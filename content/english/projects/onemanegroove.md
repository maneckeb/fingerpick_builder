## Music to the Streets! 

Wandering through downtown Salt Lake City?  Keep an eye and ear out for the unmistakable beat of Burl and his One Man Groove. A passion project of Burl's that keeps him feeling young at heart.  Busking around Salt Lake City, a true one-man band who turns sidewalks into stages and strangers into fans.

## The Maestro of Multitasking

Wearing a smile wider than the Wasatch Range, Burl straps a full drum kit to his back, slings a quattro across his chest, and somehow manages to play the kazoo and harmonica all at once, without missing a beat. It's street performance wizardry that delights audiences of all ages.

## Songs That’ll Make You Laugh Out Loud
His playlist? Think clever, funny tunes with cheeky lyrics and irresistible choruses. Sing along to feel good songs that make us remember the joy in life. Kids dance, parents chuckle, and even the most hurried pedestrian finds themselves pausing to enjoy the show.

## Available for Private Events
Beyond the sidewalks, this one man band is available to bring musical magic to birthday parties, local festivals, farmers markets, community events, and more. Looking to spice up your next gathering with some genuinely offbeat, crowd-pleasing entertainment? He's got you covered with beats, laughs, and unforgettable moments.  

## Catch Him Around Town
Whether you're on Main Street grabbing coffee or exploring the arts district, keep your ears open and your camera ready. The rhythm, the laughter, and the sheer spectacle are hard to miss.  Bring your Kazoo and join in the fun!

## Salt Lake City Gospel 

A group of Salt Lake City area musicians came together to create a gospel album as a tribute to a grandmother.   Possibly all of our grandmothers.   One of the several brain childs of Daniel Young and possibly the first album recorded at <a href=https://www.orchardstudio.org/>Orchard Studio</a> on vintage analog gear.   

## Instruments Played:
Banjo, Tenor Banjo

## Genre Bending Music

Mario Slice is eccletic and experimental music spanning multiple genres with elements of Indie Rock, Jazz, Synth-pop, Lo-Fi, Reggae, Electronic, and more.  

Mario Slice's music is full of quirky beats, layered instrumentation, and introspective lyrics. The sound is often described as genre-fluid, with a DIY aesthetic and a playful, sometimes surreal vibe.

If you're into artists who defy categorization and enjoy sonic experimentation, Mario Slice is worth exploring.
